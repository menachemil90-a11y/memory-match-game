Memory Match ("the app") is a memory game for families, published by
[PUBLISHER NAME] ("we"). We built it to collect as little as possible.

## Summary

- **Your photo boards are stored only on your phone.** The only exception is your own choice to play an online game on one of your photo boards (see "Online games on your photo board"): then only the few small cards that game needs are sent, visible only to you and the friend who joins, and deleted when the game closes — always within 24 hours.
- **No account, no ads, no tracking.** The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
- **Online play is optional** and uses only a nickname, an avatar and the moves of the game. There is no chat.

## What stays on your device

Photos you add to custom boards (and the cropped card images made from them),
board names, best scores, game history used to avoid repeating card layouts,
player names for 2-player mode, and your settings. This data is stored in the
app's private storage. We cannot see it. Deleting a board removes its photos;
uninstalling the app removes everything.

The app asks for camera access only when you tap "Take a photo". Photos are
chosen with the system photo picker, so the app cannot browse your gallery.

## Online play

When you choose **Play Online**, the app connects to our game server (hosted by
Supabase, Inc. as our service provider) and processes:

- a random anonymous ID created for your device (no email, phone or name is needed);
- the nickname and avatar you choose (nicknames are filtered for inappropriate words);
- the game itself: room code, board, level, your moves and the score;
- only if the host chooses a photo board: the small game cards described below;
- technical data necessary to deliver the service, such as your IP address in server logs.

The game server is located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

We use this data only to run online games. We do not sell or share it, and we
do not use it for advertising. Data is encrypted in transit.

**Retention:** game rooms are deleted automatically a few hours after the game
ends; anonymous IDs are deleted after 30 days without online play.

## Online games on your photo board

Only the player who creates an online game (the "host", who has the app) can
choose one of their own photo boards for it. Before anything is sent, the app
asks for confirmation. If the host confirms:

- the app sends **only the cards that game needs** (one per pair, e.g. 3 to 18) —
  small, cropped, re-compressed copies (about 480 × 480 pixels, without the
  original photo's metadata such as location) — never the original photos or
  the rest of the board;
- they are stored in a **private** storage area of our game server (Supabase,
  Frankfurt, Germany). There are **no public links**: only the two players in
  that game room can view them, through links that expire after two hours;
- they are **deleted automatically when the game room closes**, and **always
  within 24 hours** of being sent, even if the room is still open. The host's
  app also deletes them right away when the host closes the room;
- the friend who joins **never uploads anything**. Their app shows the cards
  only during the game and does not keep them in its storage.

Please only use photos you are happy to show the friend you invite, and do not
use photos of other people without their permission.

Online play is for playing with people you know — share your game code only
with friends and family.

## Children

The app is designed for all ages, including children. It contains no ads, no
in-app purchases, no chat and does not ask for personal information. We comply
with Google Play's Families policy and applicable children's privacy laws
(such as COPPA and GDPR-K). If you believe a child has provided personal
information through a nickname, contact us and we will delete it.
