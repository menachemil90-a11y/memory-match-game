# Phone shot list (real app, Menachem's phone)

Two screens exist only in the Android app and can't be filmed in a browser: choosing
photos from the gallery and the crop screen. Video 1 (`family-photos`, scene 2) uses a
motion-graphics version of that moment until these recordings exist. Everything else in
the videos is already real footage of the game.

## Before recording

- Copy the 8 demo photos from `promo/photos/` (01–08, AI-generated, fictional people) to
  the phone, into their own album (e.g. "Demo"), so the gallery shows only them. Don't
  record any real family photos.
- Do Not Disturb on, so no notifications appear; full battery or charging (no low-battery
  icon); hide the status bar clock if the phone allows it.
- Sound effects on, music off (Settings in the game).
- The app's latest version (in-app update first).
- Record with the phone's built-in screen recorder, portrait, highest quality, **no
  touch dots** and **no microphone** (the videos use the game's own sounds only).
- Slow, deliberate taps; wait about 1 second between steps. Each shot can be its own
  recording. Start each one 2 seconds before the first tap and stop 2 seconds after.

## A. Choosing photos (replaces scene 2 of `family-photos`) — ~10 s

1. Home → **My Boards** → **Create board** (the "New board" screen).
2. Board name: type **Our family** (in Hebrew too if you like: **המשפחה שלנו** — record
   both if possible: one for each language version).
3. Tap **Choose photos** → the phone's gallery/photo picker opens → open the "Demo"
   album → tap the 8 photos one by one in order 01 → 08 (the checks should be visible)
   → confirm (Done / Add).
4. Let the "Preparing photos…" screen finish; stop recording 2 s after the photos appear.

## B. The crop screen — ~8 s

1. Continue from A (or edit the board → tap a photo → **Crop again**): the **Crop
   photos** screen with photo 02 (Grandma baking).
2. Pinch to zoom in slowly on Grandma's and the girl's faces, drag a little to center
   them, pause 1 s.
3. Tap **Next** once (photo 03), then **Done all**.
4. Stop 2 s after the board is saved.

## C. The board in the game (optional, nice to have) — ~10 s

1. From the board: **Play solo** → **Medium** (8 pairs).
2. Let the deal finish; flip two different photos (a miss), then find Grandma's pair.

## Sending

Send the recordings as files (not through a chat app that re-compresses them), e.g. to
Google Drive, and name them `A-gallery.mp4`, `B-crop.mp4`, `C-board.mp4` (add `.he` /
`.en` if the board name differs). I'll cut them into the phone frame with the same
captions and re-render; the motion-graphics scene stays as a fallback.
